The choice

You decide to find other survivors and make your way to the surface. You climb up the stairs and see a massive alien ship hovering above the city. You see a group of humans running towards the ship, you join them and they lead you to a secret entrance. You enter the ship and find a group of humans who have been living there for years. They welcome you and offer you food and shelter. You are safe for now, but you know that the aliens will be back soon.
